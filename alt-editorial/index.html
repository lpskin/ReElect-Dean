Why I'm Running

This town raised me. I want to keep it worth staying in.

I've served on the Hermosa Beach City Council for four years, and it's been an honor to represent the best little beach city in the world.

I ran because I love this town — its beach culture, its small-town atmosphere, and its people. I'm engaged in that culture every day, and I believe it matters to have a council member who is truly part of it.

Over the last four years I've focused on protecting what makes Hermosa special, cutting red tape so businesses can thrive, and making sure residents feel good about calling Hermosa home. I've also experienced firsthand the public safety and traffic challenges facing our community — it's an issue that's dear to my heart, and I'm committed to improving safety for residents, businesses, and visitors.

As we enter a period of tighter budgets, we have to be more fiscally accountable than ever. I'll keep pushing for greater transparency and fiscal discipline at City Hall, so every dollar is spent wisely and every voice is heard.

Platform

Three priorities for the next term

See the full issues page →

Public Safety & Traffic

Traffic and safety issues touch every resident, business, and visitor in Hermosa. I'm committed to concrete improvements — not just talking points — on the streets and intersections that worry people most.

Fiscal Accountability

Tighter budgets ahead mean the city has to be disciplined. I'll keep pushing for real transparency in how the city spends, so residents can see every dollar accounted for and every decision explained.

Protecting What Makes Hermosa, Hermosa

Growth and red tape shouldn't come at the cost of the small-town beach character that brought most of us here. I want to cut the friction that hurts local businesses while keeping the town recognizable to the people who love it.
