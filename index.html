Why I’m Running for Re-Election

I’ve served on the Hermosa Beach City Council for four years, and it’s been an honor to represent the best little beach city in the world.

I ran because I love this town — its beach culture, its small-town atmosphere, and its people. I’m engaged in that culture every day, and I believe it matters to have a council member who is truly part of it.

Over the last four years I’ve focused on protecting what makes Hermosa special, cutting red tape so businesses can thrive, and making sure residents feel good about calling Hermosa home. I’ve also experienced firsthand the public safety and traffic challenges facing our community — it’s an issue that’s dear to my heart, and I’m committed to improving safety for residents, businesses, and visitors.

As we enter a period of tighter budgets, we have to be more fiscally accountable than ever. I’ll keep pushing for greater transparency and fiscal discipline at City Hall, so every dollar is spent wisely and every voice is heard.

Dean

My Priorities
01

Public Safety

  • Pioneered stronger e-bike enforcement, including increased fines and confiscation—first in the region.
  • Approved illegal camping regulations.
  • City Council delegate to the League of California Cities; appointed by the League President to the Transportation, Communications, and Public Works Policy Committee’s E-Bike Task Force, working on statewide legislation; and Santa Monica Bay Restoration Commission representative as the South Bay Council of Governments delegate.
02

Fiscal Stewardship

  • Only councilmember to put taxpayers first by opposing the sales tax hike and upper-management bonuses.
  • Reduced wasteful consultants, including those promoting taxes; streamlined management; and refurbished City Hall and beach bathrooms rather than pursuing multimillion-dollar rebuilds.
03

Small Business Advocacy

  • Led efforts to cut red tape, activate vacant commercial spaces, streamline permitting, reduce outdoor dining fees, and preserve convenient free parking for customers.
04

Protecting Hermosa Beach

  • Only councilmember who protected residential parking zones, holiday parking, Lot E parking, and morning free parking.
  • Improved the Housing Element, stopping increased building heights.
  • Fought for short-term rental taxes.
  • Led the countywide ban on Coastal Commission advertising waivers to protect beaches and was the only councilmember opposing Coastal Commission VIP beach waivers.
  • Sole candidate with early endorsements from the Sierra Club and California League of Conservation Voters.
05

Accountable Leadership

  • Protected public comment time, reinstated community forums, and fought for civility and professionalism at City Hall.

I’m your independent voice—ensuring civility without barriers and asking hard questions to keep Hermosa Beach safe, financially responsible, business friendly, and environmentally protected.
